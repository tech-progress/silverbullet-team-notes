# Upgrade and rollback

Template SemVer describes services/variables/storage/authentication; upstream software versions are separate pins. Current contract **1.0.2** corrects documentation only; runtime, upstream pins, authentication, storage and deployment defaults are unchanged from v1.0.1. Incompatible storage/auth/source changes require a new template major version. No runtime migration is introduced here.

Keep a stopped-writer full-root archive and stable settings, validate the new slim image on a copy, then change its digest and rerun two-account access, revisions, attachment, restart and restore tests. Never switch to legacy single-space mode as an upgrade shortcut.

Before upgrading: stop ingestion/writes, retain encrypted off-volume backups and generated secrets, record image digests, and verify a restore to an isolated clean volume. Preserve one replica and all mount paths. After upgrading: run the full meaningful smoke, examine migration/worker logs, verify anonymous/cross-account denial and retained bytes, and audit the exported template contract. Do not use mutable `latest` tags or automated major updates.

Historical sanitized source [tech-progress/silverbullet-team-notes](https://github.com/tech-progress/silverbullet-team-notes) has immutable `v1.0.1` at `dc2fd4b53169da7e9fa4eb57e37d376ae8288d7d`; its `main`/`release-v1` channels were verified at that commit. Preserve immutable v1.0.0/v1.0.1 tags. For future v1.0.2 release, audit the sanitized source and create a new immutable version/tag, then move the real `release-v1` channel only for the compatible correction. Requalify that exact source commit and stored draft graph on Railway before marketplace publication; historical v1.0.1 tests do not qualify v1.0.2. Source-release or offline verification alone does not establish live qualification or publication. Downgrades without pre-migration snapshots are unsupported.
